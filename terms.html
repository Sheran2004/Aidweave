Last updated: 12 November 2026

These starter terms are for the Aidweave MVP. Each live operator must review and publish terms suitable for the countries where it operates before opening registration.

Who can use Aidweave

You must be at least 18 years old and able to enter into an agreement where you live. Keep your account credentials private and provide an email address you control. Organization accounts must submit accurate public verification information.

Use the service respectfully

Share genuine requests and offers. Do not impersonate another person, threaten or harass anyone, discriminate, share another person's private information, submit fraudulent or unlawful content, or misuse private messages. Do not post exact addresses, phone numbers, financial details or sensitive records on the public board. Report suspicious activity to the moderators.

Safety and matching

Aidweave connects community members but does not verify that every request, helper or organization is genuine. An organization badge means the operator reviewed submitted information; it is not a guarantee of conduct. Use judgment, keep coordination in the private conversation, and meet in public places where appropriate. Never send money or sensitive documents because of a request on this service without independent verification.

Aidweave is not an emergency service and does not provide medical, legal, financial or professional advice. Contact local emergency services for immediate danger.

Moderation and account closure

Moderators may hide reported content, reject organization verification or disable accounts that violate these terms or create safety risks. You can delete your account in the dashboard. Deletion removes your requests and messages; minimal anonymized audit records may remain for abuse prevention.

Availability and responsibility

The service is provided as-is under the open-source MIT License. The operator may pause or change the service. These terms do not promise that help will be found or delivered. Nothing here removes rights that cannot legally be waived in your jurisdiction.

Contact and governing terms

The operator must publish a monitored contact address and region-specific governing terms before launch. This project does not yet have a legal operator or a public contact address.
