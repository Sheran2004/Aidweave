# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want Aidweave to be a welcoming space where people can collaborate respectfully across backgrounds, abilities, identities and countries.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind, patient and respectful.
- Listen to people whose experiences differ from yours.
- Give feedback about ideas and code, not a person's worth.
- Respect privacy. Do not share another person's identifying or sensitive information.
- Keep discussions focused on making the project more useful and safer.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized attention, and deliberate exposure or misuse of personal information are not acceptable.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the project maintainers privately. A public reporting contact has not yet been established; until then, avoid sharing sensitive personal information in public project discussions.

Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when needed to protect the community. This code is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
