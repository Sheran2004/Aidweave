Last updated: 12 November 2026

This notice describes the Aidweave open-source MVP. A person or organization that deploys Aidweave is responsible for operating the service, setting contact details and retention periods, and complying with laws where it operates. This project does not yet have a service operator or privacy contact.

What the service stores

Account email, display name, role, country or region, and (for organizations) organization name. Requests include their text, category, city or region, urgency, visibility and status. Private messages, invitation email addresses, organization verification evidence, request reports, account-security tokens and moderation audit events may also be stored.

How it is used

To operate accounts, verify email, publish public requests, deliver private invitations, coordinate a matched request, prevent abuse, and review reports and organization verification. The MVP does not process payments or sell personal data.

Public and private information

Public requests can be read by anyone and display a generic community-member label (or an organization name), request details, category and city or region. Do not include phone numbers, email addresses, exact addresses, financial details, or another person's identifying information in a public request. Private requests are limited to the requester, the verified invited account, the matched helper, and moderators who need to review a report. Conversations are limited to the requester and matched helper.

Email and security

The service operator's configured Resend or SMTP email provider delivers verification, password-reset and private-invitation links and processes recipient email addresses and one-time links for delivery. Passwords are stored as scrypt hashes. Production session cookies are HttpOnly, SameSite=Lax and Secure. Individual database fields are not encrypted by the MVP.

Retention and deletion

Account holders can delete their account from the account dashboard. Requests and messages they created are removed; requests assigned to them are reopened; organization evidence and invitations are removed. Their profile is disabled and anonymized. A minimal audit reference may remain to support abuse investigations. The operator should publish retention periods for moderation records and backups.

Age, sensitive needs and emergencies

Aidweave is for adults aged 18 and over. It is not an emergency, medical, legal, financial or identity-verification service. Contact local emergency services for immediate danger. Do not submit real medical records, payment details or sensitive information about another person.

Contact and changes

A live operator must replace this paragraph with a monitored privacy contact before accepting public requests. For operator setup and the complete data inventory, read PRIVACY.md in the source repository.
